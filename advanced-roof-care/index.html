      Should I restore my roof or replace it?

      Many tiled and Colorbond roofs that look tired are structurally sound, and a restoration (repairs, repointing and a fresh coating) can bring them back. Replacement makes sense when the roofing itself has failed. Rob will tell you honestly which applies once he's inspected it.

      How quickly can I get a quote?

      Quotes typically go out within a day or two of Rob seeing the roof.

      My ceiling has a water stain. Is it urgent?

      Get it looked at soon. A small leak can damage plaster, insulation and timber well before it drips. Common causes are cracked or slipped tiles, failed pointing, rusted flashings and blocked valleys. If water is coming in, call rather than email.

      What's the difference between rebedding and repointing?

      The ridge caps along the peaks and hips of a tiled roof sit on a bed of mortar, sealed with pointing. Repointing renews that flexible outer seal; rebedding replaces the mortar bed underneath when it has cracked or come loose. Many older Melbourne roofs need both.

      Can faded Colorbond be resprayed?

      Yes. Colorbond that has chalked or faded in the sun can be cleaned, prepared and resprayed to bring the colour back, often without replacing the sheets, as long as they're sound. Bayside homes see more fading and salt exposure, so it's a common job here.

      Can you match my existing tiles?

      Rob will look for matching or compatible replacement tiles for your roof. On older roofs exact matches can be hard to find, and he'll talk you through the options before any work starts.

      Do you repair Laserlite pergola roofs?

      Yes. Rob has repaired Laserlite pergola roofs, including replacing cracked or discoloured sheets and fixing leaks.

      How long does a roof restoration take?

      It depends on the size and condition of the roof and the weather, since coatings need dry days. One customer had their roof finished five days after the quote. Rob will give you a realistic timeframe and a start date he keeps.

      Do I need to be home while the work is done?

      Usually not for roof work, as long as Rob has safe access to the roof and outside taps or power where needed. You can sort out the details when you book.
