Service 01 · Restore

Roof restoration, back to its best.

When the structure is sound but the surface has had its day, restoration can bring your existing roof back to good shape.

Book a free on-roof quote ↗(08) 6158 9911 →

What it is

Fix what's there, before you replace it.

Roof restoration works with the roof you already have. Perth sun, heat and winter storms wear a roof down over time, and restoration addresses that wear across the whole surface rather than one leak at a time.

It isn't right for every roof. That's why we start with a free inspection on the roof itself: if restoration will do the job, we'll say so, and if a re-roof makes more sense, we'll explain why.

  • Signs it may be time

  • Faded, chalky or worn roof surface
  • Cracked or slipped tiles, or loose ridge capping
  • Leaks or water stains on ceilings after rain
  • Moss, lichen or built-up dirt across the roof
  • You're preparing to sell and the roof lets the house down

What it is

A new roof, start to finish.

Re-roofing takes off the old roof and puts a new Colorbond roof in its place. It's the answer when the existing roof is past restoring, or when it's made of asbestos sheeting you'd rather not live under.

Asbestos to Colorbond

Older asbestos cement roofs replaced with modern Colorbond sheeting.

Tile to Colorbond

Concrete or terracotta tiles replaced with a lighter Colorbond roof.

  • Signs it may be time

  • Your home has an original asbestos cement roof
  • Tiles are cracking, porous or repeatedly being replaced
  • Leaks keep returning after repairs
  • The roof is beyond what a restoration can fix
  • You want the look and low upkeep of Colorbond

Service 03 · Replace

Colorbond roof replacement.

When a metal roof is rusting, leaking or simply worn out, new Colorbond sheeting gives you a clean, solid roof again.

Book a free on-roof quote ↗(08) 6158 9911 →

What it is

New sheeting, not more patches.

Colorbond roof replacement swaps old, tired metal roofing for new Colorbond. It's for roofs where patching rusted sheets or chasing leaks has stopped making sense.

We'll look at the sheeting, fixings and flashings on the roof and tell you whether replacement is the right call, or whether a smaller repair will do for now.

  • Signs it may be time

  • Rust spots, holes or corroded sheet laps
  • Loose or rusted screws and fixings
  • Leaks you've patched more than once
  • Lifting sheets or noisy movement in wind
  • Faded, worn sheeting that dates the whole house

Service 04 · Gutters

Guttering & fascia, the roof's working edge.

New gutters and fascia that catch the water, protect the eaves and finish the roofline neatly.

Book a free on-roof quote ↗(08) 6158 9911 →

What it is

Where the roof meets the rain.

Gutters collect everything the roof sheds, and fascia boards carry the gutters and close off the eaves. When either fails, water ends up where it shouldn't: down walls, into eaves and around footings.

We replace gutters and fascia on their own or as part of a re-roof, so the edge of the roof matches the rest of it.

  • Signs it may be time

  • Gutters overflowing, sagging or pulling away
  • Rust, holes or leaking joints along the gutter line
  • Rotting, flaking or warped fascia boards
  • Water marks or stains on walls below the eaves

How the job is done

  1. 01

    Inspect the roofline

    We check gutters, fascia and how water leaves the roof.

  2. 02

    Remove the old

    Failing gutters and damaged fascia are taken off.

  3. 03

    Fit new fascia and gutters

    New fascia and gutters go up, set to fall towards the downpipes.

  4. 04

    Connect and check

    Downpipes are connected and the run is checked so water drains away.

Service 05 · Drainage

Downpipes & soakwells, water sorted.

Getting rainwater off the roof is half the job. Downpipes and soakwells get it safely away from the house.

Book a free on-roof quote ↗(08) 6158 9911 →

What it is

From gutter to ground.

Downpipes carry water down from the gutters; soakwells, common across Perth's sandy soils, let that water soak into the ground away from your footings. If either is blocked, undersized or damaged, the water has nowhere to go.

We look at the whole path, from gutter to soakwell, so a new roof isn't let down by old drainage.

  • Signs it may be time

  • Water pooling near the house after rain
  • Gutters overflowing even when they're clean
  • Cracked, rusted or disconnected downpipes
  • Downpipes that empty straight onto paths or garden beds
  • Soakwells that fill and overflow in heavy rain

How the job is done

    Install

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

What Perth homeowners usually ask before a re-roof, restoration or gutter job.

Still unsure?

Ask Irwan on the roof.

The quickest way to a real answer about your roof is a free inspection.

Is the quote really free?

Yes. Quotes are free, and Irwan gets on the roof to look at it properly before quoting.

Should I restore my roof or re-roof?

It depends on the roof's condition and what it's made of. A structurally sound roof with a worn surface can often be restored. A roof with asbestos sheeting, failing tiles or widespread rust is usually better replaced. We'll tell you what we see during the inspection and explain the options.

Can you replace an asbestos roof with Colorbond?

Yes, asbestos-to-Colorbond re-roofing is one of our core services. Asbestos removal in WA is regulated and must be handled safely, so ask us during your quote how removal will be managed for your home. Please don't disturb or pressure-clean an asbestos roof yourself.

Why change from tiles to Colorbond?

Homeowners often choose Colorbond for its clean look, lighter weight on the roof frame and low maintenance compared with ageing tiles. Whether it suits your home is something we'll talk through at the quote.

Do you offer a warranty?

Yes, our work comes with a 10-year warranty. We'll go through what it covers when you receive your quote.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Roofing Quotes WA is licensed and insured, and trades as Roofing Quotes Pty Ltd, ABN 54 158 615 466.

How long does a re-roof take?

It varies with the size and shape of the roof, the material coming off and the weather. Once we've inspected the roof we can give you a realistic idea of timing for your job.

My gutters overflow. Is it the gutters or the drainage?

It can be either. Blocked or sagging gutters, too few downpipes or a soakwell that can't keep up can all cause overflow. We check the full path the water takes, from roof to soakwell, before recommending a fix.
