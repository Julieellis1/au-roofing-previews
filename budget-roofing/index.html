      Home/FAQ

      FAQ

      Roofing questions, answered.

      General guidance for Sydney homeowners. Every roof is different, so the team will confirm what's right for yours on inspection.

      Should I repair my roof or replace it?

      It depends on the condition of the whole roof, not just the leak. Isolated damage such as a few cracked tiles or a lifted sheet can often be repaired. Widespread rust, brittle tiles, repeated leaks or an asbestos roof usually point towards a re-roof. The team can inspect and talk you through both options.

      Can you replace my tiled roof with Colorbond?

      Yes. Tile roof replacement with Colorbond is one of the jobs Budget Roofing does regularly. A metal roof is lighter than tiles and has fewer joints. The crew will check the roof frame and plan the flashings, gutters and ridge details before starting.

      My roof might contain asbestos. What should I do?

      Don't drill, cut, water-blast or walk on it. Older fibro and corrugated cement roofs in Sydney may contain asbestos, and in NSW removal is regulated work that must be done safely and to SafeWork NSW requirements. Contact the office to talk about asbestos roof removal and replacement.

      What should I do after a hailstorm?

      Check inside for water stains and leaks first, and keep off the roof. Take photos of any visible damage for your insurer. Budget Roofing repairs hail-damaged roofs, including outdoor area and pergola roofs, and can inspect to see what's needed.

      Do you replace pergola and outdoor area roofs?

      Yes. Customers have had the team replace pergola and outdoor area roofs, including hail-damaged ones, often alongside a main roof replacement.

      Do you work on commercial and industrial buildings?

      Yes. Budget Roofing works across residential, industrial, commercial, architectural and traditional projects, including new builds with engineered details. Builders can email plans to info@budgetroofing.com.au.

      Can you make custom flashings and trims?

      Fabrication is part of the business, so flashings, cappings and trims can be made to suit the roof rather than forcing an off-the-shelf part to fit.

      Which areas of Sydney do you cover?

      The team is based in Padstow and works across Sydney, including Canterbury-Bankstown, St George, the Sutherland Shire, the Inner West and the South West. See the service areas page or just ask.

      How do I get a quote?

      Use the quote form, email info@budgetroofing.com.au or call (02) 9793 8255. Include your suburb, the type of roof and what you'd like done. Photos help too.

      What are your office hours?

      Monday to Friday, 7:30 am to 5 pm. Quote requests by email can be sent any time.
