Home / Services / Roof replacement

Roof replacement for roofs past patching.

When repairs no longer make sense, the old roof comes off and a new one goes on, with the house kept protected throughout.

What it is

Out with the old roof, in with a sound one.

A replacement removes the existing roofing and installs a new roof in its place. It is the right call when a roof keeps leaking in new places, the material has reached the end of its life, or you are renovating and want the roof to match.

Signs you may need it

  • Leaks keep returning after repairs, or appear in several rooms
  • Widespread rust, cracked or slipping tiles, or brittle sheets
  • Sagging lines or water damage in the ceiling
  • You are renovating and the roof no longer suits the house

Not sure whether to repair or replace? An inspection will tell you honestly.

How the job is done

A replacement planned around the weather.

  1. 01Inspect and adviseDaniel checks the roof and explains whether replacement is really needed.
  2. 02Plan the changeoverScope, timing and how the house stays covered if rain comes in.
  3. 03Strip and replaceThe old roof is removed and the new roof installed in stages.
  4. 04Finish and tidyFlashings and details completed, the site cleared, a final walk-through.

Home / Services / Repairs & leaks

Roof repairs and the tricky leaks others couldn’t find.

Water travels before it drips. Daniel’s team traces a leak back to its source and fixes the cause, not just the stain on the ceiling.

WhatsApp a photo of the leak Call 0420 222 609
Representative image · not a verified Gauci project

What it is

Find the source. Fix it properly.

Repairs cover anything from broken tiles and lifted sheets to failed flashings, valleys and gutters. Leak detection is the detective work first: where the water shows inside is often a long way from where it gets in.

Signs you need a repair

  • Water stains, drips or bubbling paint on ceilings after rain
  • Damp or musty smells in the roof space or upstairs rooms
  • Cracked, slipped or missing tiles, or loose roof sheets
  • Rusted or lifting flashing around chimneys, skylights and walls
  • Overflowing gutters or water running down walls

How the job is done

From first drip to dry ceiling.

  1. 01Tell us what you seeCall or WhatsApp photos of the stain, drip or damage.
  2. 02Trace the leakThe roof and roof space are inspected to find where water really enters.
  3. 03Repair the causeFlashing, tiles, sheets or seals repaired or replaced as needed.
  4. 04Explain what was doneYou hear what failed, what was fixed and anything to watch.

Frequently asked

Before you call.

Still unsure? Ask us directly →

Should I repair my roof or replace it?
It depends on the roof’s age, material and how widespread the problems are. A single failed flashing or a few broken tiles is usually a repair. Leaks in several places, widespread rust or brittle material often point to replacement. An inspection gives you an honest answer before you spend.
How do you find a leak that keeps coming back?
Water often enters well away from where it shows on the ceiling, running along battens or sarking first. The team inspects the roof and roof space, checks flashings, valleys, penetrations and gutters, and traces the path back to the source so the real cause is fixed.
What should I do while my roof is leaking?
Move valuables and electrical items away from the drip, catch water with a bucket, and keep out of the roof space if the ceiling is sagging or wet near light fittings. Don’t climb on a wet roof. Then call or WhatsApp photos to 0420 222 609.
What are the signs my roof needs attention?
Ceiling stains, damp smells, bubbling paint, cracked or slipped tiles, rust on metal sheets, lifted flashing and overflowing gutters are all worth checking. After a big Sydney storm or hail, it pays to have the roof looked at.
Do you work with builders and commercial clients?
Yes. As well as homeowners, Gauci Roof Installation works for builders and commercial clients on new roofs, replacements and repairs. See the builders & commercial page.
What happens if it rains during my roof job?
Sydney weather changes fast. Work is planned around the forecast and adjusted mid-project when conditions change, with the priority on keeping the property protected. Clients have specifically mentioned the team adapting plans to the weather.
Are you licensed?
Yes. Gauci Roof Installation Pty Ltd holds NSW contractor licence 390183C (ABN 86 646 627 661). You can check any NSW licence on the NSW Government’s licence check service.
Which areas do you cover?
Sydney-wide from Woolloomooloo, including the Inner City, Eastern Suburbs, Inner West, North Shore, Lower North Shore and South Sydney. See service areas.
